## Notes

- 12 of 14 mainline entries are fully playable on XBATOCERA today.
- The two gaps (Three Houses, Engage) are both Switch-exclusive — out of scope for the current setup. Yuzu and Ryujinx are both legally defunct as of last year, and XBATOCERA's integrated graphics (OptiPlex 3040) wouldn't handle Switch emulation well regardless. Would require a separate project if ever pursued.
- If you just want to play one game for the best graphical experience actually available: **Radiant Dawn** (Wii, via Dolphin) — full 3D models and cutscenes, confirmed running well on the hardware.
- If you'd rather start fresh with the game most people consider the modern gateway to the series: **Awakening** (3DS).
- Path of Radiance is running from the Japanese ISO with a fan translation patch (`Fire Emblem - Souen no Kiseki [T-En by Shadowofchaos]`), not the official US GameCube release — functionally complete either way.
- Fates entries were originally miscounted as a gap during the initial audit — all three paths (Anya Oukoku/Conquest, Byakuya Oukoku/Birthright, Special Edition/Revelation, all fan-translated as "If") were already present.
